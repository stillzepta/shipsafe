# Xiaohei IP Reference — Ian Xiaohei Illustrations

## Visual Identity & Anatomy

Xiaohei ("Little Black") is an iconic visual protagonist designed for editorial and technical clarity:

```
      ( o.o )    <-- Simple round or bean-shaped solid black head with two white dot eyes
      / | | \    <-- Minimalist stick/silhouette arms and legs
     [#######]   <-- Pitch-black silhouette body (flat #000000)
      /     \
```

- **Color**: Pitch-black solid silhouette (`#000000`).
- **Facial Features**: Exactly two tiny white circular dot eyes. No mouth, no nose, no eyebrows (expression is determined by eye spacing, head tilt, and physical posture).
- **Limbs**: Slender, expressive silhouette limbs capable of holding tools, levers, magnifying glasses, shields, or clipboards.
- **Demeanor**: Deadpan, earnest, slightly stoic, focused entirely on the absurd yet technically coherent mechanical task at hand.

## Behavioral Principles

1. **Active Participation**:
   - Xiaohei must *never* just stand in the corner waving or smiling like a passive brand mascot.
   - Xiaohei must physically operate the machinery, hold up a magnifying glass to inspect a syntax error, intercept an exposed secret key with a wrench or shield, or pull a giant lever that diverts bad code into a quarantine bin.

2. **Absurd Yet Coherent Metaphor**:
   - The scene should feature physical, mechanical representations of software concepts:
     - Pre-commit hook -> An oversized industrial barrier gate with a scanner eye.
     - Secret leak -> A radioactive-looking glowing key or credential trying to slip through a slit.
     - Debugger statement -> A literal bug with antennae trapped under a glass beaker.
     - SQLite Audit DB -> A neatly organized vault of index cards or ledgers.

3. **Scale & Context**:
   - Xiaohei typically appears at human scale relative to miniature code objects, or tiny relative to giant server machinery, emphasizing the developer's guardianship over complex systems.
