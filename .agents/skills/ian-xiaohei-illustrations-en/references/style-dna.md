# Style DNA — Ian Xiaohei Illustrations

## Visual Hierarchy & Constraints

### 1. Canvas & Background
- **Aspect Ratio**: Strict 16:9 widescreen landscape (`1920x1080` or `1280x720`).
- **Background**: Absolute pure white (`#FFFFFF`). Never use off-white, cream, parchment textures, grey fills, gradients, or soft drop shadows.
- **Whitespace / Negative Space**: Generous breathing room. The core subject and linework must occupy only **40% to 60%** of the entire canvas. Never crowd edges.

### 2. Line Art & Ink Style
- **Technique**: Black ink line drawing with fine, continuous contours and subtle organic jitter/wobble, resembling a felt-tip pen or technical pen on white bristol board.
- **Lineweight**: Consistent fine-to-medium strokes (2px–3px equivalent) for structures, with slightly thicker solid fills for Xiaohei's silhouette.
- **Hatching & Shading**: Minimalist sparse diagonal cross-hatching or stippling. No smooth airbrush or gradient shading.

### 3. Color Palette Restraint
- **Base (90%)**: Pure Black (`#000000` / `#111827`) linework on Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Accents (10% max)**: Strictly restrained to three specific functional accent tones:
  - 🔴 **Red (`#EF4444`)**: Danger, critical errors, blocked actions, leaked secrets, reject paths.
  - 🟠 **Orange (`#F97316`)**: Warnings, friction points, mechanical gears, caution notices.
  - 🔵 **Blue (`#3B82F6`)**: Clean safe flows, approved routes, production release, data streams.
- **Taboo**: Never use pastel rainbow palettes, decorative purple/pink gradients, or flat corporate vector fills.

### 4. Handwritten English Typography & Callouts
- **Style**: Hand-drawn sans-serif lettering with slight organic tilt, looking authentically hand-annotated.
- **Content**: Extremely terse keyword labels, state tags, and dynamic arrow annotations (e.g., `"STAGED CODE"`, `"SECRET LEAK!"`, `"QUALITY GATE"`, `"PROD READY ✔"`).
- **Taboo**: No multi-sentence narrative paragraphs inside the image. The illustration is a cognitive map, not a slide transcript.

### 5. Explicit Anti-Patterns & Taboos
- ❌ No generic corporate isometric illustrations (the "SaaS corporate Memphis" style).
- ❌ No 3D glossy shapes, plastic spheres, or glassmorphic gradients.
- ❌ No cute cuddly mascots or chibi cartoon eyes.
- ❌ No decorative background floating doodles that don't serve the technical metaphor.
