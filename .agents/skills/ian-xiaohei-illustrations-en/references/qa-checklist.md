# QA Checklist — Ian Xiaohei Illustrations

Before finalizing and embedding an illustration, perform this quality verification:

- [ ] **Canvas Ratio**: Is it strictly 16:9 widescreen landscape?
- [ ] **Pure White Background**: Is the background absolute `#FFFFFF` without grey drop shadows, beige tints, or paper grain?
- [ ] **Whitespace**: Does negative space make up at least 40%–50% of the canvas?
- [ ] **Linework Quality**: Are the lines black, crisp, fine, and subtly hand-drawn rather than glossy or overly digital?
- [ ] **Character Integrity**: Is Xiaohei a pitch-black solid silhouette with two simple white dot eyes and no mouth?
- [ ] **Character Engagement**: Is Xiaohei actively engaged in a purposeful physical task rather than passively posing?
- [ ] **Color Discipline**: Are colors strictly limited to sparse functional accents in Red (`#EF4444`), Orange (`#F97316`), and Blue (`#3B82F6`)?
- [ ] **Typography**: Are handwritten English callout labels concise, legible, and directly tied to technical concepts?
- [ ] **Metaphor Coherence**: Does the physical metaphor accurately explain the underlying software architecture?
